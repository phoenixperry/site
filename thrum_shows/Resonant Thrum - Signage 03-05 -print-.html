This space is being recorded

Resonant Thrum is a research study. While it runs, the group is photographed, audio- and video-recorded, and each participant's heart-rate (biofeedback) data is collected and anonymised for research and academic presentation.

Taking part is voluntary · you may withdraw at any time. If you'd prefer not to be recorded, please let a facilitator know before your session.

Resonant Thrum is a guided experience. It runs as facilitated 10-minute sessions for small groups of 2 to 6, not as a walk-in installation, so it sits quiet between sittings.

To take part, book a slot and come back at your session time. A facilitator will bring your group in at the allotted time.
