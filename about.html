Cult Hero

Phoenix Perry is an artist, researcher and activist who has spent more than 25 years making technology that brings people together. They are Reader in Games and Creative Technologies at the UAL Creative Computing Institute in London, where they designed the MSc and BSc in Creative Computing, and Project Lead on Federated Data Commons for Creative Communities, an AHRC-funded project with New York University.

Their work spans games, embodied interaction, accessible design and creative AI, and keeps coming back to who gets to shape technology and how play can make it fairer. Phoenix sees building schools, studios and communities as a kind of social fiction. What world do we want? How do we get there from here?

Highlights

  • Has secured about £1.23 million in research funding from the AHRC, SSHRC, EPSRC, UKRI, Google and Epic Games. In 2026 Phoenix became Project Lead on two AHRC-funded projects, with UAL as lead organisation: The Antigravity Machine, a UK and Canada collaboration co-funded by SSHRC, and Federated Data Commons for Creative Communities (AHRC BRAID).
  • Best Paper Award at ACM CHI PLAY 2026 for Cripping the Controller, with new 2026 papers at NIME and ACM Creativity & Cognition. More than 15 peer-reviewed papers and contributions to four books, including Meet the Kinect (Apress) and Code as Creative Medium (MIT Press).
  • Creator of InteractML, open-source interactive machine learning for Unity and Unreal, used by more than 60,000 developers.
  • Founder of the Code Liberation Foundation, which taught creative computing to 6,000 women (2013 to 2021), and owner and artistic director of Devotion Gallery in Brooklyn (2009 to 2014).
  • More than 70 talks and keynotes in Europe, the UK, the US, Canada and South Africa, including Future Days, KIKK, the Gotland Game Conference, IndieCade, TEDxLiège and the Sir Run Run Shaw Lecture at Stony Brook University.
  • Exhibiting since 1998: Bot Party at GDC Alt.Ctrl, Somerset House and IndieCade; Forest Daydream at the Wellcome Collection; Resident Thrum at ACM Creativity & Cognition; and early video work at Oberhausen, LACMA and Yerba Buena Center for the Arts.
  • Helped establish the UAL Creative Computing Institute and has designed and led degrees at UAL and Goldsmiths, after teaching at NYU, UCL and the Utrecht School of the Arts. Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy.
  • Serves on the ACM Games editorial board and the Processing Foundation advisory board, and is Artworks Co-Chair for ACM Creativity & Cognition 2026.
  • Before academia, 14 years as an art director and creative director in interactive design (1999 to 2013), and co-founder of the game studio Dozen Eyes in New York.

A short bio you are welcome to use:

Phoenix Perry is an artist, researcher and Reader in Games and Creative Technologies at the UAL Creative Computing Institute in London. Their work explores play, embodied interaction and fairer AI. They lead two AHRC-funded projects, The Antigravity Machine and Federated Data Commons for Creative Communities, founded the Code Liberation Foundation, created the open-source toolkit InteractML, and have given more than 70 talks worldwide. Their 2026 paper Cripping the Controller won Best Paper at ACM CHI PLAY.

Pronouns: they/them. For interviews, talks, commissions and research partnerships, email phoenix@phoenixperry.com. Photo by Julian Dasgupta.
