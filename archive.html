Earlier work, before games and research: the video art and club scene of San Francisco around 2000, and the records Phoenix made of it.

Reline

Co-founder and co-curator, 2001 to 2006

A video art DVD series Phoenix created with Scott Pagano, to keep a lasting record of artists making new visual forms with custom software and modified hardware. Reline 1 (2002) screened at the Yerba Buena Center for the Arts, the Guggenheim Bilbao and Lincoln Center; Reline 2 followed in 2006.

The Reline story

Synth

Co-founder and art and music director, 2001 to 2002

A monthly San Francisco club night for live electronic music and video art, co-founded with Brian Jackson. Kid606, Scanner, Lusine and Jonas Bering played, and it won Best Club to Hear the Future (SF Bay Guardian, 2001) and Best Pickup Spot for HAL 9000 (SF Weekly, 2002).
